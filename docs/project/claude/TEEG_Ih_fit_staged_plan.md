## 0. Abstract and scope

**Question.** The passive fit $(C_m, R_m, R_a)$ on Allen human cells rails at the $C_m$ bound for some cells and does not reproduce Allen's own $\tau_m / R_{\rm in}$. The hypothesis is that unmodelled I_h (sag) breaks the single-RC relation the passive model assumes. This plan adds an explicit I_h mechanism to the fitted model, promotes **three** I_h quantities to fitted parameters — density $\bar g_h$, the position of the activation curve $\Delta v_h$ (which sets $m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$), and a time-constant scale $\kappa_\tau$ — and fits the whole long-square step instead of its first 60–150 ms, with the larger hyperpolarising steps admitted so that the sag is a large signal rather than a nuisance.

**Covered.** Notation (§1); what exists (§2); corrections to the v1 handoff (§3); the decisions as recorded and what still needs a yes (§4); the training/validation protocol (§5); the model and parameter vector with bounds (§6); the loss (§7); the staged implementation, which is now a refactor of a copy of the monolith (§8); smoke and regression tests (§9); cost (§10); equations (§11); open points (§12); sources (§13).

**Excluded.** Phase 2.5 (dropped for this work per D-006); the Eyal-archive adapter; interneurons (no human interneuron I_h kinetics in hand; §12); the diameter re-estimation itself (an upstream input to this work, §4.3).

## 5. Training / validation protocol (D-006, confirmed by D-007 C2) — IMPLEMENTED

The archive already holds every long-square sweep of every cell (§2). Let the hyperpolarising subthreshold sweeps of a cell, grouped by amplitude, be $h_1, \dots, h_n$ in increasing $\lvert I \rvert$, and the depolarising sweeps without spikes $d_1, \dots, d_k$ (spike-free by the Allen white paper's action-potential rule since D-016: no event of the 10 kHz Bessel-filtered dV/dt reaching 20 mV/ms whose voltage reaches $-30$ mV and 2 mV above the crossing within 5 ms, and no sample above $-20$ mV, both on the archive's LJP-corrected voltage; the existing `_detect_step_amplitude` gives the amplitude). Allen's protocol starts at $-110$ pA in 20 pA steps, some low-$R_{\rm in}$ human cells at $-220$ pA in 40 pA steps [KB], so $n$ is typically 5–7 and the loader's current 100 pA cap is what has been excluding $h_n$ and sometimes $h_{n-1}$.

| Role | Bundles | Window $W_b$ | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Train** | SS hyperpolarising pulses (as today; exp time-weight, $\tau_w = 5$ ms) | 0.5–100 ms after pulse | $C_m$ — nothing else carries it |
| **Train** | $h_2, \dots, h_{n-1}$ | **step**: $[t_{\rm on}, t_{\rm off}]$ | Q6: large I_h signal, amplitude dependence of the sag; the strongest step withheld per Q6 |
| **Validate (gate)** | $d_1, \dots, d_k$ (small, spike-free) | step | Q7: I_h *de*-activation regime, never trained on |
| **Validate (gate)** | $h_1$ (smallest) | step | Q12: weak stimulus held out |
| **Validate (report only, not in the gate)** | $h_n$ (strongest) | step | Q6's worry made measurable: if unmodelled conductances act at that depth, this residual says so |
| **Diagnostic (report only)** | every long step | **rebound**: $[t_{\rm off}, t_{\rm end}]$ | I_h deactivation time course; excluded from training because PIR conductances (Na, Ca_LVA in Rich 2021's model) are not in ours |
| **Validate** | SS depolarising pulses (as today) | 0.5–100 ms | unchanged |

Two knobs keep the alternatives explicit opt-ins, labelled in the output: `--ls-window {after_onset, step, sweep}` (default `step` for the I_h arms) and `--train-all-hyp` (which is what §5 of v0.2 called `--ls-train-roles all_hyp`: it admits $h_1$ and $h_n$ to training too). A trough guard `--v-trough-min` (default off) demotes to report-only any training step whose $V_{\rm trough}(b)$ is below a set voltage; it exists for the case where $h_{n-1}$ is itself very deep in a low-$R_{\rm in}$ cell. Values are logged per cell into **`ls_roles.csv`**: which sweep played which role, why, its amplitude and its $V_{\rm trough}$.

**Both exclusions are checked, not assumed.** `ls_diagnostics.csv` carries one row per long step of every role with the RMSD over the arm's window, the three sub-window RMSDs of §7 (charging / sag / rebound), and the sag fraction, $t_{63}$ and rebound amplitude of the DATA and of the MODEL side by side.

**Gate window — a departure, recorded as D-010 (ii).** `run_biological_fit.py` computes the training side of the absolute-mV gate over the fit window but the validation side over the first 150 ms of a long step, a window whose source comment says it exists "to minimise Ih contamination in the validation signal". For an I_h arm that removes from the gate exactly what the gate is for, and it also makes `valid_to_train_ratio_abs` a ratio of quantities measured over different windows. So `passive_fullstep` and `ih6` use the same window on both sides (`gate_valid_via = "same_window"`); `baseline_runB` keeps the legacy asymmetric form, because reproducing run B window for window is its only purpose. A cross-arm comparison of `valid_rmsd_abs_mV` is therefore valid between `passive_fullstep` and `ih6`, and NOT between either of them and `baseline_runB`.

**Passive control arm.** The same table with the same windows, fitted with $\theta = (C_m, R_m, R_a)$ only. Without it, a loss improvement of the I_h arm could be the window and the admitted steps, not I_h [reasoning]. **Baseline arm** (run B, 60 ms / 2 smallest steps / 12 mV cap) is not re-run; its CSV is read.

## 6. Model and parameter vector

### 6.1 Kinetics and the two fitted kinetic knobs

Base kinetics selectable per run: `Ih_human` (Rich 2021 Eq. 1, Table 1 [KB]) or `Ih` (Kole 2006 as in Hay 2011 [src]) with an optional configuration shift $\Delta v_{\rm base}$ applied to both curves (Kalmbach 2018: rates evaluated at $v - 20$, i.e. $\Delta v_{\rm base} = +20$ mV in this convention [PMC; Rich 2021 Eq. 5 KB]). On top of the base, two **fitted** knobs, added to both `.mod` files as RANGE parameters:

- `vshift_minf` $= \Delta v_h$: $m_\infty(v) = m_\infty^{\rm base}(v - \Delta v_h)$. Shifts the activation curve only; $\tau^h(v)$ is untouched. This is the faithful implementation of "sweep $m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$" because at fixed $V_{\rm rest}$ it is a monotone map $\Delta v_h \mapsto m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$ — but it also moves $m_\infty$ at the trough, so it changes the *sag fraction*, which is what makes it identifiable at all [reasoning].
- `tau_scale` $= \kappa_\tau$: $\tau^h(v) = \kappa_\tau\, \tau^{h,{\rm base}}(v)$.

For the Kole base, $m_\infty$ and $\tau^h$ come from the same $\alpha, \beta$; the `.mod` computes $m_\infty$ from rates at $v - \Delta v_{\rm base} - \Delta v_h$ and $\tau^h$ from rates at $v - \Delta v_{\rm base}$, then scales. For the Rich base the two curves are already independent functions, so the shift enters $v_h$ and the scale enters $\tau^h$ directly. Defaults ($\Delta v_h = 0$, $\kappa_\tau = 1$) reproduce the current files bit-for-bit (regression S1).

Reference values at $V_{\rm rest} = -73.5$ mV [run]: $m_\infty$ 0.026 (Kole), 0.166 (Kole with the Kalmbach shift), 0.104 (Rich); $\tau^h$ 46 / 73 / 341 ms; peak $\tau^h$ 78 / 78 / 343 ms; $V_{1/2}$ $-110.3$ / $-90.3$ / $-90.9$ mV.

### 6.2 Distribution

`uniform` (D-005): $\bar g_h(s) = \bar g_h$ on soma, basal, apical. The axon stub is **excluded** by default (`IhSpec.regions = ("soma","dend","apic")`), the stub being a Hay artefact; `--ih-regions` can add it, labelled. `eyal_exp_323` and `hay_exp_dmax` selectable, output labelled with the law. Under a non-uniform law the reported $\bar g_h$ is the soma/basal value and the fitted values are **not** comparable with the uniform run.

### 6.3 Search box

| Parameter | Box | Scale | Anchors |
|---|---|---|---|
| $C_m$ | $[0.3, 3.0]$ µF/cm² | log | unchanged |
| $R_m$ | $[10^3, 10^5]$ Ω·cm² | log | unchanged |
| $R_a$ | $[50, 1000]$ Ω·cm | log | unchanged |

$q = (\log C_m, \log R_m, \log R_a, \log \bar g_h, \Delta v_h, \log \kappa_\tau)$. `skopt` handles the mixed scale with one linear `Real` dimension. **$\Delta v_h$ being the one linear axis is load-bearing downstream:** Phase 3's samples are carried in $q$ and converted back per axis, so a blanket `np.exp` would have exponentiated $\Delta v_h$ into a plausible, wrong CI. S8 asserts that it does not.

### 6.4 Rest handling (D-005, Q5)

Per segment, Eq. (11.3): $e_{\rm pas}(s)$ is set so that the resting h-current is cancelled by the leak at $v = V_{\rm rest}$. Then $v \equiv V_{\rm rest}$ carries no axial current and is a stationary state of the whole cable, and `finitialize` puts $m = m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$ [reasoning; cross-checked: with the Kalmbach-shifted Kole, $\bar g_h = 10^{-4}$, $R_m = 30$ kΩ·cm² it returns $-87.7$ mV, next to Kalmbach's fitted uniform-passive $-86.97$ mV [PMC, run]]. $e_{\rm pas}$ therefore has status **computed**, recomputed at every loss evaluation because it depends on $(R_m, \bar g_h, \Delta v_h)$ and on $F(s)$. Reported per cell: `e_pas_soma_mV`, `gh_rest_over_gpas` $= \bar g_h\, m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})\, R_m$, and `rest_drift_mV` — the measured $\max_t \lvert v(t) - V_{\rm rest}\rvert$ over 2 s with no stimulus, which is 0 to machine precision on every configuration tested (S3, and again per cell at the fit point).

### 6.5 Derived quantities reported alongside $\theta$

`m_inf_at_rest`, `tau_h_at_rest_ms`, `tau_h_peak_ms`, `v_at_tau_h_peak_mV`, `v_half_mV`, `gh_rest_over_gpas`, `e_pas_soma_mV`, `ehcn_mV`, `rest_drift_mV`, plus the model's sag fraction, $t_{63}$ and rebound amplitude on every long step beside the data's own (Allen's sag definition [KB]: (trough − steady)/(trough − baseline)). These are written by `param_spec.flatten_result_params` and by nothing else, so no quantity appears twice under two names.

## 7. Loss

Unchanged in form (Eq. 11.4): mean over training bundles of $\mathrm{RMSD}_b / D_b$, SS pulses time-weighted as today, long steps uniform-weighted over the step window. Sub-window RMSDs (charging 0–150 ms after onset; sag 150 ms–offset; rebound) are computed and written per bundle but **not** minimised. Known property to watch: with $W_b$ = step, the plateau dominates the sample count and constrains $g_{\rm pas} + \bar g_h m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$ as a sum; the separation of $R_m$ from $\bar g_h$ comes from the sag fraction across $h_2 \dots h_{n-1}$ and from the SS pulses [reasoning]. If Stage 7 shows $\Delta v_h$ or $\kappa_\tau$ unrecovered, the fallback is sub-window weighting (three normalised sub-windows averaged), implemented behind `--ls-subwindow-weighting`, default off.

Expected signal size, for calibration of expectations only: Moradi Chameh 2021 [KB] report L2&3 sag amplitude $1.8 \pm 1.6$ mV and the ZD7288-sensitive part $1.3 \to 0.2$ mV on their $-50$ to $-200$ pA steps; L3c $2.9 \pm 1.8$ mV. The I_h part of the signal is a few mV on deflections of 10–20 mV, which is why the larger steps matter and why the bootstrap CIs (Phase 3) are kept.

**One property of the loss that is not about I_h at all.** Both builders wrap their body in `except Exception -> P` with $P = 10^6$. $P$ is finite, so a cell whose every simulation raises returns a finite loss and a check written as "the loss is finite" passes while nothing is being exercised. This is not hypothetical: it happened in this plan's own §9 suite (D-010 (iii)). Every liveness check therefore asserts $\max L < P$ **and** that $L$ varies, and `run_ih_fit.py` runs that check per cell before the fit.

## 10. Cost

Per loss evaluation: unchanged integration length (the whole sweep was always simulated), one more state variable per segment, plus one cheap pass to recompute $e_{\rm pas}(s)$. Training bundles per cell rise from ~3 to ~6 (SS + $h_2..h_{n-1}$), so ~2× per evaluation. Budget 200/100 per fit against run B's 100/50 → 2×. Three arms (baseline read from disk, so two new fits per cell) → 2×. Net ≈ 8× run B per cell for Phase 2, before Phase 3. Phase 3 at C5's 100/30 × $B = 200$ on half the cells ≈ 20 000 local evaluations per bootstrapped cell — measure one cell in the Stage 8 dry run before fanning out. GP fitting time itself grows with dimension and with 200 points; not estimated, measured in the dry run. The dropped $\tau_w$ sweep saves $\lvert{\rm grid}\rvert \times 15$ profile evaluations per cell, which at run B's single-point grid was 15.

## 11. Equations

**11.1 Mechanism**, for each segment $s$ and each fixed $v$:
$$ i_h(s) = \bar g_h(s)\, m\,(v - E_h), \qquad \frac{dm}{dt} = \frac{m_\infty^{\rm base}(v - \Delta v_{\rm base} - \Delta v_h) - m}{\kappa_\tau\, \tau^{h,{\rm base}}(v - \Delta v_{\rm base})}, \tag{11.1} $$
with $\Delta v_{\rm base} = 0$ for `Ih_human` and for unshifted `Ih`, $+20$ mV for the Kalmbach variant; $\Delta v_h = 0$, $\kappa_\tau = 1$ reproduce the published models. Kole: $m_\infty^{\rm base} = \alpha/(\alpha+\beta)$, $\tau^{h,{\rm base}} = 1/(\alpha+\beta)$, $\alpha(v) = 0.001 \cdot 6.43\,(v+154.9)/(e^{(v+154.9)/11.9}-1)$, $\beta(v) = 0.001 \cdot 193\, e^{v/33.1}$ ms⁻¹ [src]. Rich: $m_\infty^{\rm base}(v) = 1/(1+e^{(v - v_h)/k})$, $\tau^{h,{\rm base}}(v) = f + 1/(e^{-a-bv} + e^{-c+dv})$, Table-1 constants as in `Ih_human.mod` [KB, src].

**11.2 Eyal law** (comparison only), for each apical segment $s$:
$$ \bar g_h(s) = \bar g_h \left(-0.8696 + 2.087\, e^{\mathrm{dis}(s)/323\,\mu m}\right), \qquad \bar g_h(s) = \bar g_h \ \text{on soma and basal}. \tag{11.2} $$

**11.3 Rest balance** [reasoning], for each segment $s$:
$$ e_{\rm pas}(s) = V_{\rm rest} + \frac{\bar g_h(s)\, m_\infty^{\rm base}(V_{\rm rest} - \Delta v_{\rm base} - \Delta v_h)\,(V_{\rm rest} - E_h)}{g_{\rm pas}(s)}, \qquad g_{\rm pas}(s) = \frac{F(s)}{R_m}, \tag{11.3} $$
$F(s) \in \{1, F\}$ the segment's spine multiplier. With (11.3) and $m(s, 0) = m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$, $v(s,t) \equiv V_{\rm rest}$ solves the cable equation for all $t$ before the stimulus.

**11.4 Loss**, $B$ training bundles, $W_b$ per §5:
$$ L(\theta) = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B} \frac{\mathrm{RMSD}_b(\theta \mid W_b)}{D_b}, \qquad \theta = \text{to\_physical}(q). \tag{11.4} $$

**11.6 Recovery error** (D-011), for each fixed cell $c$ and axis $i$, with $\theta_i(c)$ the injected ground truth and $\hat\theta_i(c)$ the estimate:
$$ \varepsilon_i(c) = \begin{cases} \log\bigl(\hat\theta_i(c)/\theta_i(c)\bigr), & i \in \{C_m, R_m, R_a, \bar g_h, \kappa_\tau\} \ \text{(dimensionless)},\\ \hat\theta_i(c) - \theta_i(c), & i = \Delta v_h \ \text{(mV)}. \end{cases} \tag{11.6} $$
The gate compares $\operatorname{median}_c \lvert \varepsilon_i(c) \rvert$, over the I_h-bearing cells for $i \in \{\bar g_h, \Delta v_h, \kappa_\tau\}$, to a per-axis tolerance. A log ratio is **not** available for $\Delta v_h$: it changes sign by construction and its zero is the published activation curve, a modelling convention rather than an origin.

**11.5 Liveness of a loss** (D-010 (iii)), for probe points $q^{(1)}, \dots, q^{(k)}$ differing in at least one axis:
$$ \max_i L(q^{(i)}) < P \quad\text{and}\quad \max_i L(q^{(i)}) - \min_i L(q^{(i)}) > \varepsilon, \qquad P = 10^6. \tag{11.5} $$
Finiteness alone is **not** sufficient, because $P < \infty$ by design.

## 12. Open points and caveats

- **Identifiability in six dimensions** is the risk of this design, not compute. At rest only $g_{\rm pas} + \bar g_h m_\infty(V_{\rm rest})$ is visible; $\Delta v_h$ separates from $\bar g_h$ only through the sag fraction's amplitude dependence, $\kappa_\tau$ only through the sag time course. The I_h signal in L2/3 is a few mV [KB]. **Stage 7 is a gate, not a formality**, and a parameter that fails it is frozen, not carried. The gate is now BUILT but **has not been run**: until it has, every fitted $\Delta v_h$ or $\kappa_\tau$ is a number the optimiser returned, not a measurement.
- **The Stage-7 tolerances are unowned.** $\log 1.25$, 3 mV, $\log 1.5$ and the 80 % low-rail fraction are the assistant's proposal, never a user decision (D-011's open list). They are applied to a median, and every per-cell error is written out, so changing them costs nothing — but a verdict reported without naming them is a verdict about an arbitrary line.
- **The cohort size is unjustified.** 20 cells is a round number, not a power calculation: with a quarter of them in the false-positive control, the low-rail fraction is estimated from 5 cells, on which one cell is 20 percentage points.
- **The recovery test is well-specified by construction.** `check_consistency` refuses a mismatch between the generated truth and the fitted model, so the gate measures recovery under a CORRECT model. Real cells are not drawn from our model at all; a pass therefore bounds the optimiser and the protocol, and says nothing about the kinetics being right.
- **The Phase-3 profile in $p > 3$ is an upper bound**, not the profile: the inner minimisation is a stratified Latin-hypercube draw of $M = {\rm inner}^2$ points rather than an exhaustive grid (§8 Stage 5). A half-width read off it is conservative in the sense of being too wide, never too narrow — but it is not the exact profile and must not be reported as one.
- **Cross-arm comparison of the gate columns.** `valid_rmsd_abs_mV` and `valid_to_train_ratio_abs` are comparable between `passive_fullstep` and `ih6` (same window both sides, same protocol) and NOT against `baseline_runB`, which keeps the legacy early validation window (D-010 (ii)).
- **No per-cell $\tau_w$** in any arm (D-010 (i)). Whether one is worth having in six dimensions is open; it would need `cm_profile_sweep.profile_cm` generalised to profile one named axis over $p-1$ nuisance axes, which Stage 5 did for the Phase-3 profile but not for the sweep.
- **Base-kinetics shapes** ($k$; the $\tau^h(v)$ bell) are inherited from an L5 fit (Rich) or a rat fit (Kole). Moradi Chameh 2021's statement that L2&3 and L5 activation time course and $V_{50}$ are "similar" is from their main text; the numbers are in a supplement not in the KB.
- **Post-inhibitory-rebound conductances** are excluded by windowing (step only) and by withholding $h_n$; the rebound residual and the $h_n$ residual are reported in `ls_diagnostics.csv` so the exclusion is checked, not assumed.
- **`nseg`**: harmless for `uniform`; re-check before trusting any Eyal-law comparison (the smoke fixture raises `nseg` to 41 for exactly this reason).
- **dt 0.1 ms** is inherited from run B by decision; the code's own note that it was to be validated before use stands.
- **Diameter-corrected morphologies**: contract settled (D-013: one SWC per specimen, archive format, `--swc-dir`, refusal on a missing file); $F$ unchanged at 1.9 for L2/L3. **Stage 7 runs on the raw arbours**, and its verdict is about the arbours it was run on: corrected diameters change how much membrane is electrotonically far enough out to sag. Re-running the gate on the corrected SWCs before the corrected-scope campaign is recommended [reasoning], not decided.
- **Noise (D-012, D-014).** The *fast* per-sweep noise is measured per protocol and injected at each cell's own acquisition. Open: (a) **whether AR(1) is enough** — the table's `acf_*` against `ar1_*` at 1 ms answers it on the real cells; if the measured value sits well above $\hat\rho^L$, the generator needs a second timescale (a new decision). [updated 2026-10-19: answered NO on L3_exc (D-016), and a second, slow timescale added per cell (D-017) [log].] The literature describes neuronal voltage and current noise with more than one spectral component (Jacobson et al. 2005; Diba et al. 2004 — both **PubMed, abstract only**, rat preparations, qualitative use only). (b) The *slow* terms (per-sweep offset, drift) are the benchmark's nominal values scaled by $\hat\sigma_{\rm LS}/0.05$ mV — an extrapolation. [updated 2026-10-19: replaced, for every cell in the noise table, by D-017's per-cell law, the tilt's SD calibrated per cell from the SS window means (D-034); not yet implemented [log].] (c) The Long Square amplitude set is not twinned. [updated 2026-10-21: D-073 needs it twinned -- each twin LS sweep the twin of one real LS sweep, same amplitude; the cohort set `--ls-hyp-amps` would go (the assistant's proposal, D-073 comment (d)) [log].] (d) The LS pre-window is assumed free of the Allen test pulse because the Phase-0 writer slices by `index_range` [src]; not verified — on the cluster, $\hat\sigma_{\rm LS} \gg \hat\sigma_{\rm SS}$ on the same cell would be the signature. ~~The noise table has not yet been run on the real archive.~~ [corrected 2026-10-19: it was run on L3_exc on the cluster -- `noise_L3.csv` 2026-09-23, `noise_L3_v2.csv` 2026-09-28 (D-017, Sources) [log].]
- **Interneurons**: excluded; both mechanisms are pyramidal-cell fits, and D-003's interneuron passive tables are themselves provisional.
- **Preprint literature**: unsearched — the bioRxiv connector has no keyword search and its date-window query errored on 2026-09-21. Unchanged as of 2026-09-23.
