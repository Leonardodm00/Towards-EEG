- S7 (Block 46, Parameters). The start's PROVISIONAL constants are not listed as parameters (F4).
- S8 (Blocks 44, 46). Both windows are closed, \|k dz - z\| <= half. The spec does not say whether a rounding tolerance applies; the two blocks differ (F7).
- Observation, not a finding: the partition model with a per-slab blur can darken a pixel below its own column's transmittance. At T8's soma (radius 6 um, mu 0.3 /um) min tau is 0.0045 against exp(-mu 2c) = 0.027 for the central column. Light from the defocused peripheral columns of the upper slabs spreads into the centre. SPEC already states "no lower bound imposed (the model does not clip)". A denser soma could reach tau < 0. Where this matters for real somata (mu about 0.2 /um, 4 um) is for the project to judge.
