## Problems in the spec
- S1 (Block 4 amendment 4, D-275's numbers): the text says the pilot table's spline "is negative for |delta| < 0.130 um, with minimum -6.06e-4 um^2 at 0.090 um". Recomputed with `CubicSpline` on the stated table, it is negative for |delta| < 0.138 um, with minimum -6.07e-4 um^2 at 0.092 um. The unfloored width there (0.0761 um) agrees. These are small digit errors in the text and change no oracle.
- S2 (Block 54 R4; mathematical): R4 says a larger theta_3 "leaves the focus plane's within 1e-3" without a size of the change. The not-a-knot spline is global, so the knot at 0.84 um moves sigma_r inside [0, 0.28] um. On a trial table (0.08, 0.12, 0.33, 0.55), theta_3 = 0.55 -> 0.75 raises sigma_r(0.10) from 0.0800 to 0.0834 um, and a 0.3 um tube's focus plane moves by 3.0e-3, beyond R4's bound. The oracle holds for steps of about 0.02 um, the smoke test's step. The same table is floored (D-275) over |delta| <= 0.15 um, so sigma_r there does not depend on theta at all until the spline crosses 0. Two consequences follow for the near stage. First, theta_3 is partly identified by the in-focus planes, so the near stage's unknowns are coupled through the spline. Second, the objective has kinks where the floor switches on or off, which matters for the finite-difference Jacobian. Worth a sentence in the common definitions.
- S3 (Block 10.C and 10.F after Block 15's amendment): Block 10.C still reads "Far: the 24 planes k_tr + o, far_min_k_offset <= |o| <= P_far", and 10.F's parenthesis still reads "(|k - k*| >= 5)". With 4 these become 26 planes and ">= 4" (F1).
- S4 (Common definitions / Block 55): the SPEC says which real planes enter the objective ("the real scan's status is ok and its omega finite"). It is silent on whether the twin's scan applies the gof screen of Block 10.G (F2), and on what a twin plane's NaN means for the objective. Block 56's NaN rule (PROVISIONAL) gives one answer, but its consequence, that the in-focus planes can drop out depending on the start, is not stated.
- Note: the clustered covariance is marked **[textbook, from memory]** in Block 56. The tester checked it numerically against statsmodels' CR0 (OLS and WLS), which agrees to 1e-8, but did not check it against a literature full text.
