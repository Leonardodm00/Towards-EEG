## Problems in the spec
- **S1** (Block 24 oracle). "the lower of two stacked tubes books less than alone, by the factor e^{-mu_1 l_1} of the upper tube's column". With `light_direction` = +1 (the default; section 2.3: light along +s, s_z > 0, so toward +z) the tube attenuated is the upper one, in z. The test checks the tube later along the light, for both directions. The wording should say "later along the light".
- **S2** (Block 24 oracle). "light reversed equals depth mirrored [1e-10]" holds only when the slab lattice is mirrored too, that is when dzeta divides the depth range. On the default lattice the two renders differ by 1.7e-5 (partition) and 2.5e-5 (hybrid), which is the depth quadrature. The smoke test sets dzeta for this; the entry does not say so.
- **S3** (Blocks 29 -> 23). Block 29's `predict` is "the node's features set to (d, mu), moved by v0 ... and Delta z", and `offset_xyz` gives "the image-frame offset a features row takes". `with_rows` replaces `offset_um`. If `predict` is built on the current featured scene, already offset, then v0 is an increment, and writing `offset_xyz` as the row's offset discards the earlier offset. The entries do not say which.
- **S4** (Blocks 27 vs 25/28). The shared definition makes a neighbour any segment "that does not belong to node i's own stretch". Block 27 counts as own every segment with both ends in `branch.ids`, and `cell.stretch_branch` prepends the stretch's parent node, so the segment to the parent is own for the survey. Blocks 25 and 28, given the stretch's ids, make it a neighbour (subtracted, or tier 2). Which `branch` the survey receives is not specified.
- **S5** (section 6). The entry-point table lists no command for Blocks 23-31: their nine smoke suites, and the "Minimal run", are absent.
- **S6** (Block 1). The entry is not updated for `CleaningConfig` and `InferenceConfig`: its Outputs line, its `validate()` refusals and its "JSON round trip is identity" do not say whether the two sections are covered (F3, F4).
- **S7** (Block 25). The base entry still states one kernel per column, the depth bins (Custom code, `shadow_depth_bin_um`), the 0.03/0.15 bounds and the depth-bin oracle. Amendment 3 supersedes them, but the superseded text is not marked in place.
- **S8** (Block 30). "a `forward` returning NaN ends `failed`" does not say whether this covers NaN met during the fit as well as at the start (F1).
- No in-scope entry is marked `Confirmed: inferred`.
