## Oracles derived from the spec
Written from SPEC 2.10 and the Block 23-28 entries before the implementation was read.

- **Block 23** (2.10 (a)):
  - r_{b,s} = sqrt(a_{b,s}) d_{b,s}/D_b, where a_{b,s} = c_b w_b(t_s)/sum w_b, and d is the difference of the two traces, each minus its own pre-window mean (ends included). The simulated trace is interpolated linearly on the recorded samples.
  - c_b under `relative` is 1/n. Under `ls_amplitude` it is 1/(n_SS+n_LS) for a brief bundle and n_LS omega_b/(n_SS+n_LS) for a long one, so sum c_b = 1.
  - Per bundle, sum r^2 = c_b (RMSD_b/D_b)^2 (`bundle_rmsd`).
  - L = sum_b c_b [sum r^2/c_b]^(1/2) equals the scalar loss of 2.8. L <= sqrt(L2).
  - Layout: masks, pre_masks, v_exp, D and slices in the training order and in time order.
  - A failed point (an exception other than dt enforcement, or a non-finite trace) gets the constant row sqrt(l_fail/N), so L2 = l_fail = 1e6, and it is counted. A DtEnforcementError reaches the caller under that class name.
  - Pooled values equal the serial ones exactly. After a call the parent's cell is at Q[-1].
  - The listed refusals; `make_residuals` refuses `equal` and `custom`. The loss's own values are unchanged.
- **Block 24** (2.10 (b)):
  - Forward: (f(u + delta e_i) - f(u))/delta, with delta = -h when u_i + h > 1.
  - Central: (f(u+h) - f(u-h))/(2h), with the forward fallback per axis.
  - Point order: axis by axis, plus before minus. Exactly one batch call. u is the first row only when f0 is needed and not given.
  - d/dq = d/du / (hi - lo).
  - A linear map is exact. Scipy's `approx_derivative` with the same absolute step is a reference implementation.
  - The listed refusals.
- **Block 25** (2.10 (c)):
  - Starts by 2.9 (b) (`select_starts`), then scipy trf on [0,1]^p with xtol 1e-4, ftol = gtol 1e-8 and max_nfev 30 by default.
  - Jacobian: forward differences with h 1e-3, one call of p rows when f0 is reused.
  - Every computed residual vector is recorded with its L2. The estimate is the lowest recorded L2 (ties: the earlier start, then the earlier evaluation).
  - improved means l2 < l2_search, with record 0 being the first start = the search's best point. best_start is -1 when not improved.
  - converged means status in 1..4. jtj = J^T J in q at the solver's endpoint; sv are the singular values of J, in descending order.
  - With no start: the search's point, n_sims 0.
  - All points lie inside the box. Deterministic.
  - Closed-form linear least-squares minimum; a minimum outside the box lies on the face. wrap: the same object, `get_residuals` called once after the minimizer, `fun` = L, or func(x) when L is NaN.
- **Block 26** (2.10 (d)):
  - J = sqrt(a) S/D on W_b.
  - r_eta = -sqrt(a) eta_tilde/D, where eta_tilde is the noise minus its own pre-window mean; a constant offset therefore does not enter.
  - dq = -(J^T J)^{-1} J^T r_eta. sd with ddof 1; corr = corrcoef. Their expectation is the sandwich A^{-1} J^T Sigma J A^{-1}, not the naive weighted Fisher inverse.
  - SVD: sigma descending, V rows the right singular vectors (largest component positive), ratio = sigma_p/sigma_1. A collinear pair gives a vanishing sigma_p.
  - Sensitivities: S in q units (chain rule); one evaluator call that includes the base point; RuntimeError when a point returns None. The npz round trip works without pickle.
- **Block 27**:
  - Arms: trf for ih5, ih6_d207 and passive_fullstep; none for ih6 and baseline_runB. CLI defaults: max_nfev 30, h 1e-3, check_h 1e-2.
  - Bound flag: u < 0.01 gives "lo", u > 0.99 gives "hi".
  - Column order and identities: `loss_at_estimate == train_rel_loss == loss_after_refine`; sqrt_l2_at_estimate^2 = l2_after_refine; `l2_after_refine <= l2_after_search`.
  - Sensitivity mode: four files with the stated keys; W0/W1 rows; fd_step_check; loss and sqrt(L2) at theta equal to the fit's eval-theta values (same protocol); `layout_from_stored` == `residual_layout` of the live bundles.
  - --sensitivity-theta outside the closed box exits 2.
- **Block 28**:
  - A long bundle's noise is the mean over its sweeps of `ls_sweep_noise`.
  - A brief bundle's noise is the mean over every stored window of its polarity of the rows of `ss_sweep_noise` of the window's sweep.
  - Seeds: 1000000 + k and the twin's id.
  - A grid mismatch raises BundleGridError.
  - merge gathers the tables (twin_id first, one per twin) and exits 0 with sensitivity files only.
  - extras gain the new columns plus on_bound_<name> for the run's arm.
  - Same seeds, same numbers.
