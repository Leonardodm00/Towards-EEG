- O14.1 (2.8 (b)): h_1..h_n are the hyperpolarising bundles sorted by |I|, and m = ceil(n/2). Train is {h_i : 2 <= i <= n, i != m}; validate is h_1 and h_m. - O14.2 (2.8 (d)): t_pk is the argmin (argmax when depolarising) over the grid samples in [t_on + 1 ms, t_off] of a centred boxcar mean. The boxcar reaches samples outside the interval and averages the finite samples it covers. Ties go to the earliest sample. Expected values come from an explicit loop with `numpy.nanmean`; a parabola's vertex is exact to the sample. Spikes 0.1 ms long do not move it. A monotone trace gives the end of the interval. A dip inside the 1 ms guard is reached by the boxcar. Refusals: a brief bundle, and fewer than 3 samples in the interval.
- O14.3: w_b(t) is 0 before t_on, (t - t_on)/(t_pk - t_on) up to t_pk, and exp(-(t - t_pk)/tau_d) after it. So w = 0 at onset, 1 at the peak and e^-1 at t_pk + tau_d. ValueError unless t_pk > t_on and tau_d > 0.
- O14.4: RMSD_b = [sum w d^2 / sum w]^{1/2} on the baseline-subtracted traces in the window, with the window modes `step`, `sweep` and `after_onset`. With `"none"` and the default, the RMSD and the deflection are bit for bit those of the base commit's `bundle_rmsd`. The deflection is never weighted. The weight depends only on the recorded trace.

A zero total amplitude, a bad option or an unusable peak is refused when the loss is built. LP6: with the defaults the loss equals the base commit's, bit for bit.
